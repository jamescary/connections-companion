# Connections Companion — Privacy Policy

Connections Companion does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any
user data.

All functionality runs locally in your browser, and only on the New York
Times Connections puzzle page. The only data the extension stores is its own
panel position and collapsed state, saved in your browser's local storage on
your device. The extension makes no network requests and has no servers.
